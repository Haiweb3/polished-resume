# Template Notes

The first template is intentionally conservative and based on a strong technical-resume layout:

- centered header
- blue section titles with divider lines
- dense but readable body copy
- A4 page size
- one-page bias

## Layout assumptions

- Works best for technical resumes with 4 to 6 sections.
- Supports Chinese and English mixed content.
- Assumes the renderer sends already-curated content.

## What to change later, not now

- alternate templates
- dark themes
- sidebar layouts
- photo-heavy variants
- social-media cover variants

## When content overflows

Adjust content first:

- cut low-value awards
- shorten skills
- merge similar bullets
- reduce project detail

Only after content cleanup should you consider layout adjustments.
